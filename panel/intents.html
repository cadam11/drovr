drovr intents

This page is drovr's inbox. Leave a note on it and the next supervisor tick reads it, applies it, replies in the thread saying what happened, and resolves the thread. The tick runs every 10 minutes, so a change lands within one. The live picture is on the panel.

Sending one

Hover any commentable block on this page, click the comment control that appears at its edge, and paste a line of the form below. Anything that does not start with drovr-intent v1 is ordinary conversation: drovr leaves it alone, unread and unresolved. 

loading…

copied

That example is deliberately inert: a zero delta against a real program exercises the whole grammar — prefix, verb, program, delta, stamp — and changes nothing, so you can paste it to prove the loop works without altering what drovr does. Every action that does something is in the table below.

The expires= stamp is required and must be ISO 8601, in the future, and no more than 24 hours out — that is what bounds a reopened thread being applied a second time. The stamp above is generated an hour out when the page loads, and again each time you press Copy, so what you paste is current even on a tab left open overnight. Do not hand-write one from memory.

Every action, and what it will refuse

drovr refuses anything out of range rather than clamping it, and tells you so in the thread. Deltas are signed and relative to the current setting.

WriteRangeWhat it does
mode <name> off · low · normal · away How much of the pool programs may spend. off stops new cycles entirely.
pause <n>h 1h – 72h No cycle starts until it elapses, whatever the mode. Replaces any pause in force.
reserve <delta> −0.95 – 0.95 Moves the share of quota held back for you, against the mode in force. Refused while the mode is off.
concurrency <delta> −7 – 7 Cycles allowed to run at once across all programs. Lands in 1–8.
enable <program>
disable <program>		 a program in control.json Whether that program is considered at all. Names the state, so a repeat is not a flip back.
priority <program> <delta> −9 – 9 Lower wins the next capacity slot. Lands in −9 – 9.

Interrupting a running cycle is deliberately not here — drovr never kills a cycle. Use drovr watch and press x, which sends an interrupt so the runner still winds down and makes its work durable.

What comes back

  • Applied. The thread gets a reply naming the new setting, and is resolved.
  • Refused. Same thing, with the reason — out of range, no such program, the stamp missing or stale. The thread is still resolved: an unreadable note left open would be retried on every tick forever.
  • Left alone. Not an intent, so not drovr's business.
  • Resolved with no reply. Rare, and it means the notes call failed midway. Check the thread before assuming: drovr resolves a thread before it changes anything, so a lost instruction is possible and a repeated one is not.

Only notes written by craig are applied. Anyone else who can read this page gets a refusal naming that as the reason.

If drovr's log says this page is unreadable, shared notes are off on it. Only the page owner can turn them on, from the notes widget here — never from the CLI.
